03 / The idea

The rides keep moving.
The questions keep coming.

At Apoloplex, apologists evangelize among the passing crowds. A visitor stops. Someone asks a question. And a perfectly ordinary day at the theme park takes a philosophical detour.

The comics unfold in full-size 3:2 scenes—as many as each story needs—with exaggerated faces, cluttered surroundings and explanatory notes below every image. The apologists defend the Christian perspective; Phil and other visitors challenge their claims and ask follow-up questions.
